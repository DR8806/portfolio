On the rear anti-roll bar mounts, the nut plates in the previous design interfere with the inverter. The inverter will be basically permanently in the car, so we need a way to torque the bolts from one side, the outside. Because of this and the tight tolerance, we decided to use HiLoks, specifically the HL18PB6-18 from Monroe Aerospace. The part number breaks down as:

SymbolMeaning
HL18Pin basic part number: Hi-Lok pin, protruding shear head, alloy steel
PBFinish code: cadmium plate (Type II, Class 2) with cetyl alcohol lube
6Nominal diameter in 1/32 in: 6/32 = 3/16 in
-Separates the diameter and the max grip
18Max grip in 1/16 in: 18/16 = 1 1/8 in
